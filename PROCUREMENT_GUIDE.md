# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Enterprise Procurement & Purchasing Guide

## Overview
The Z-FINANCE Enterprise Procurement System provides end-to-end purchasing workflow management, from initial purchase requisition to 3-way invoice matching and automated General Ledger posting.

## System Features
1. **Purchase Requisitions (PR)**: Multi-level departmental approval chains.
2. **Requests for Quotation (RFQ)**: Multi-vendor competitive bidding.
3. **Purchase Orders (PO)**: Digitally signed purchase contracts with SHA-256 verification.
4. **Goods Receipt Notes (GRN)**: Quality inspection and partial/full stock receipt recording.
5. **3-Way Matching**: Automated validation between PO, GRN, and Supplier Invoice.
6. **Supplier Contracts & SLAs**: Framework agreements with auto-renewal alerts.
7. **Offline Support**: Local caching for offline purchase requests with background synchronization.
