# Service Pricing Guide - Z-FINANCE 1.0.0

## 1. Overview
The **Service Pricing Engine** supports versatile financial models suitable for all service industries.

---

## 2. Supported Pricing Models
1. **Fixed Price**: Set static fee per engagement.
2. **Hourly Rate**: Rate per billable hour.
3. **Daily Rate**: Fee per work day.
4. **Weekly Rate**: Standard weekly retainer.
5. **Monthly Rate**: Monthly billing cycle.
6. **Annual Rate**: Yearly contract.
7. **Negotiable Price**: Custom quotation per client request.
8. **Free Service**: Pro-bono or community service.
9. **Subscription Service**: Recurring plan integration.
10. **Quotation Required**: Formal proposal workflow required.

---

## 3. Multi-Currency Support
Supports base currency codes (`USD`, `XAF`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `CAD`, etc.) with real-time conversion hooks.
