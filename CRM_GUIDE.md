# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Enterprise CRM Guide

## Overview
The Z-FINANCE CRM ecosystem provides 360° customer relationship tracking, lead scoring, contact history, credit limits, and full integration with Billing, Accounting, Projects, Communication, EDMS, and AI Assistant.

## Core Capabilities
1. **CRM Dashboard**: Real-time tracking of active leads, total customers, sales pipeline value, active subscriptions, open tickets, and customer lifetime value (CLV).
2. **Lead Prospecting & Capture**: Multi-source lead intake, automatic lead scoring (1-100), qualification stages, follow-up logs, and lead-to-opportunity conversion.
3. **Customer 360° Management**: Detailed customer profile, multi-contact management, credit limit controls, industry taxonomy, and linked documents.
4. **Offline Support**: Local lead queuing and customer record caching with background synchronization.
