# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Multi-Vendor Marketplace Guide

## Overview
Multi-vendor marketplace infrastructure allowing external sellers to list products, process sales, and request commission payouts.

## Capabilities
- **Vendor Stores**: Independent seller storefronts and dashboards.
- **Commission Rates**: Configurable platform commission percentages (e.g. 15%).
- **Payout Ledger**: Automated accounting GL posting upon commission payout approval.
