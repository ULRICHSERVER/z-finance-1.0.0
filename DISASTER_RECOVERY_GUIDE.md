# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity Plan

## Overview
Comprehensive disaster recovery protocol featuring Point-in-Time Recovery (PITR), database rollback, encrypted backup validation, and automated failover orchestration.

## Recovery Objectives
- **RPO (Recovery Point Objective)**: < 15 minutes.
- **RTO (Recovery Time Objective)**: < 1 hour.
