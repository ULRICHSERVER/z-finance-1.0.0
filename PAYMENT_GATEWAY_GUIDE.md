# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Payment Gateway Integration Guide

## Overview
Unified payment processor connector supporting traditional bank card terminals, online payment gateways, and African mobile money rails.

## Supported Gateways
- **Card Processors**: Stripe, PayPal, Flutterwave, Paystack.
- **Mobile Money**: MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, Express Union Mobile Money, PayUnit.
- **POS Terminals**: Serial/USB card reader terminal integration.
