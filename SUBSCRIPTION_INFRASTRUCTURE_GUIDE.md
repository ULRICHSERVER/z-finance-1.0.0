# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Subscription Infrastructure & Billing Guide

## Overview
Automated SaaS subscription plan management supporting Starter, Professional, and Enterprise Unlimited tiers with usage quota enforcement.

## Supported Billing Models
- **Monthly Recurring Billing**
- **Annual Recurring Billing** (20% discount default)
- **Lifetime Enterprise Licensing**
- **Free Trial** (14-day auto-converting trial)
