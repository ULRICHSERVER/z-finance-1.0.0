# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 — Payment Methods Guide

## Supported Payment Gateways & Channels

Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 supports regional mobile payment providers alongside global electronic settlement rails:

1. **Mobile Money (CEMAC & West Africa)**:
   - MTN Mobile Money (MoMo)
   - Orange Money (OM)
   - Express Union Mobile Money (EUMM)
2. **Local Payment Aggregators**:
   - PayUnit
3. **Banking & Traditional**:
   - Cash
   - Bank Wire / SWIFT / SEPA
   - Cheque
4. **Cards & International Gateways**:
   - Credit Card / Debit Card
   - Stripe Integration (API Ready)
   - PayPal Integration (API Ready)
   - Flutterwave (API Ready)
   - Paystack (API Ready)
