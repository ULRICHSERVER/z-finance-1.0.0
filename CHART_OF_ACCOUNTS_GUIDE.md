# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 — Chart of Accounts Structure Guide

## Overview

The **Chart of Accounts** (`chart_of_accounts` table) establishes the standard numeric ledger numbering convention:

- **1000s - Assets**: Current Assets, Bank Accounts, Accounts Receivable, Inventory, Fixed Assets.
- **2000s - Liabilities**: Accounts Payable, Sales Tax Payable, Short/Long-Term Debt.
- **3000s - Equity**: Owner Capital, Retained Earnings, Dividends.
- **4000s - Revenue**: Service Sales, Product Sales, Subscription Revenue, Other Income.
- **5000s - Direct Costs (COGS)**: Hosting Costs, Freight, Direct Delivery Costs.
- **6000s - Operating Expenses**: Rent, Utilities, Software SaaS, Marketing, Payroll.
