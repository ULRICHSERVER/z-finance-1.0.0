# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Maintenance & Servicing Management Guide

## Overview
Automated maintenance scheduling for machinery, IT equipment, vehicles, and tools to prevent unplanned downtime.

## Maintenance Types
- **Preventive Maintenance**: Scheduled recurring inspections and servicing.
- **Corrective Maintenance**: Repairs triggered by equipment breakdown.
- **Calibration**: Precision tool & laboratory instrument alignment.
- **Cost Tracking**: Record estimated and actual repair costs.
