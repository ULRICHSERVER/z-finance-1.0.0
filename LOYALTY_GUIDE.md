# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Loyalty & Rewards Program Guide

## Overview
Customer retention system with points accumulation, tier progression, cashback vouchers, and milestone rewards.

## Mechanics
- **Tiers**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum.
- **Points Accrual**: Automated points per € spent on billing invoices or POS transactions.
- **Redemption**: One-click redemption for checkout discount vouchers.
