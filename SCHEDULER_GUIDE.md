# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Cron Scheduler Guide

## Overview
Manage minute, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and custom cron expressions for automated enterprise batch jobs.

## System Jobs
1. **Weekly Executive Summaries**: Auto-compilation and distribution of PDF financial reports.
2. **Nightly POS Register Reconciliation**: Balancing multi-branch cash drawer balances at end-of-day.
3. **Monthly Payroll Processing**: Automated batch tax calculation, payslip generation, and bank transfer queueing.
