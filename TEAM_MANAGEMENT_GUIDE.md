# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Team Management Guide

## Overview
Enables multi-level grouping of staff into agile teams, project squads, operational units, and department subdivisions.

## Core Capabilities
- **Team Structure**: Assign Team Leaders, Department Parentage, and Member Rosters.
- **Cross-Functional Units**: Create custom teams across multiple departments.
- **REST API Endpoint**:
  - `GET /modules/teams/api/teams.php`
  - `POST /modules/teams/api/teams.php`
