# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Delivery Fleet & Order Dispatch Guide

## Overview
Dispatch management for local delivery zones, driver assignments, delivery fee calculations, and proof of delivery tracking.

## Features
- **Zone Management**: Define distance-based delivery zones.
- **Driver Dispatch**: Assign couriers and track delivery status in real time.
- **Proof of Delivery**: Digital signature capture and GPS timestamp logging.
