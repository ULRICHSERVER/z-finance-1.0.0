# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 — Revenue Tracking & Analytics Guide

## Revenue Formulas & Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Total Gross Income**: Sum of all received, completed, and partially received income entries converted to the organization's base currency (e.g., XAF).
   $$\text{Total Gross Income} = \sum (\text{Amount} \times \text{Exchange Rate})$$

2. **Net Revenue**: Gross Income minus direct income refunds, cancellations, and transaction fees.

3. **Income Growth Rate**:
   $$\text{Growth Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Current Period Income} - \text{Prior Period Income}}{\text{Prior Period Income}} \right) \times 100$$

4. **Income Source Performance**: Percentage contribution of each specific income source relative to the entire income portfolio.

## Recurring Revenue Engine
Configure automated schedules with execution policies:
- **Daily**: Micro-transactions and daily service feeds
- **Weekly**: Retainers and weekly subscriptions
- **Monthly**: Rent, server hosting, monthly contracts
- **Yearly**: Software licenses and annual maintenance agreements
- **Custom Intervals**: Flexible day-count schedules
