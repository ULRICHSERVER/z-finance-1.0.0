# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - AI Smart Automation Guide

## Overview
Automated workflows triggered by financial events, document uploads, low stock alerts, and recurring cron schedules.

## Supported Workflows
1. **Invoice Auto-Categorization**: Document AI automatically categorizes uploaded supplier invoices and matches appropriate General Ledger accounts.
2. **Unusual Expense Alerting**: Real-time detection of expense spikes (+100% over moving average) with instant administrator notifications.
3. **Automated Procurement RFQ**: Automatic creation of procurement requests when warehouse stock drops below defined reorder levels.
4. **Scheduled Executive Reports**: Automatic PDF report compilation and email distribution on set schedules.
