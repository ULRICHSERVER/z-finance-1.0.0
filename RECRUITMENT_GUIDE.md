# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Recruitment & Applicant Tracking System (ATS) Guide

## Overview
Talent acquisition pipeline management from job posting to candidate evaluation and offer letter issuance.

## Features
- **Job Positions**: Open/Close vacancies with target salary ranges.
- **Applicant Tracking**: Stage movement (Applied -> Screening -> Interview -> Offered -> Hired).
- **Interview Scheduling**: Integrated calendar link and scoring matrix.
- **Background Check**: Qualification document upload and verification.
