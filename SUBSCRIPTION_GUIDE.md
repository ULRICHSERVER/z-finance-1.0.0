# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Subscription & Recurring Billing Guide

## Overview
Manage recurring SaaS, service, and product subscriptions across flexible billing cycles with automated invoice generation.

## Capabilities
- **Flexible Plans**: Free, Trial, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual, and Lifetime plans.
- **Lifecycle Actions**: Pause, Resume, Upgrade, Downgrade, Cancel, and Auto-Renew.
- **General Ledger Sync**: Auto-posts recurring billing revenue to Accounting GL.
