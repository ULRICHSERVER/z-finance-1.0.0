# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Requests for Quotation (RFQ) Guide

## Overview
The RFQ engine manages multi-vendor bidding, supplier quotation collection, and comparative evaluation.

## Workflow
1. **Initiation**: Requisition items are grouped into an RFQ with closing dates and specs.
2. **Supplier Dispatch**: Automated portal/email invitation dispatched to qualified suppliers.
3. **Sealed Bids**: Supplier quotes remain encrypted until the RFQ deadline.
4. **Weighted Matrix**: Auto-scoring based on price (40%), lead time (30%), rating (20%), and payment terms (10%).
