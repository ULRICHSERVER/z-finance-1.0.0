# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Multi-Level Approval Workflow Guide

## Overview
Enterprise multi-stage approval routing for high-value financial transactions, purchase orders, leave applications, and contract sign-offs.

## Approval Types
1. **Single Approval**: Direct supervisor sign-off.
2. **Multi-Level Approval**: Department Head -> Finance Director -> Executive Director hierarchy.
3. **Delegation & Escalation**: Automatic escalation after 48-hour timeout.
4. **Digital Approvals**: Logged audit trails with Super Administrator override tracking.
