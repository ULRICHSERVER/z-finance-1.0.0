# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Customer Support & Helpdesk Guide

## Overview
Ticketing system with multi-category routing, priority queues, SLA escalation timers, and Knowledge Base FAQ sync.

## Capabilities
- **Ticketing**: Open, In-Progress, Escalated, Resolved, Closed ticket states.
- **SLA Engine**: Automated warnings for tickets approaching SLA response limits.
- **Customer Portal**: Direct ticket submission and status tracking for clients.
