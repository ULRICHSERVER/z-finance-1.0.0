# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Supplier & Vendor Management Guide

## Overview
Vendor Qualification and Onboarding engine ensuring corporate compliance and risk mitigation.

## Features
- **Registration**: Tax registration validation, ISO certificates, and banking details.
- **Risk Assessment**: Categorization into Low, Medium, and High risk profiles.
- **Portal Self-Service**: Vendors update profile data and view payment statuses directly.
