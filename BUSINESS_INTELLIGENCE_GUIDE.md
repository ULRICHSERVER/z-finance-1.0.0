# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Business Intelligence & Financial Forecasting Guide

## Overview
Predictive machine learning algorithms and business intelligence dashboards for executive decision-making.

## BI Capabilities
1. **Financial Regression Forecasting**: 30-day, 90-day, and 1-year revenue, expense, and cash flow predictive modeling with confidence scoring.
2. **Inventory Demand Forecasting**: Sales velocity analysis predicting future stock reorder dates.
3. **Customer Conversion Insights**: CRM funnel velocity tracking and lead growth predictions.
4. **Expense Optimization Engine**: AI detection of redundant SaaS subscriptions and vendor pricing anomalies.
