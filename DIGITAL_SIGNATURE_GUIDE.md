# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Digital Signature & Approval Workflow Guide

## Overview
Legally binding digital signatures integrated directly with EDMS workflows.

## Features
- **Signature Modes**: Drawn canvas signatures, typed signatures, uploaded image stamps, and cryptographic certificates.
- **Verification Hash**: Generates unique SHA-256 verification hashes recording signer name, email, timestamp, and IP address.
- **Approval Chains**: Multi-step workflow chains (Review -> Legal -> Executive Approval).
