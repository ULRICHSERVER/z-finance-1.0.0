# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Enterprise Point of Sale (POS) Guide

## Overview
The Z-FINANCE Point of Sale (POS) terminal provides touchscreen, barcode scanner, QR scanner, and offline transaction capabilities across physical retail, branch networks, and pop-up counters.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Touchscreen POS Checkout**: Fast product/service grid search, quantity modification, and barcode scan integration.
2. **Flexible Payments**: Support for Cash, Card Terminal, MTN/Orange Mobile Money, Gift Cards, and Split Payments.
3. **Receipt Printing & Cash Drawer**: Automated thermal receipt printer triggers and cash drawer opening signals.
4. **Offline Mode**: Sales transactions are safely saved to local storage during internet outages and synchronized automatically when online.
