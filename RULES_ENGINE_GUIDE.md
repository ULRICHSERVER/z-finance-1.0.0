# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Rules Engine Guide

## Overview
A high-performance conditional decision matrix for evaluating business logic, thresholds, and role-based permissions.

## Rule Structure
- **Fields**: `amount`, `date`, `status`, `role`, `department`, `branch`, `customer`, `supplier`, `inventory_level`.
- **Operators**: `equals`, `greater_than`, `less_than`, `contains`, `in_list`.
- **Logic Groups**: `AND`, `OR` nesting with AI-assisted rule recommendations.
