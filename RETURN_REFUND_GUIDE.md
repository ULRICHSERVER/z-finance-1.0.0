# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Returns & Refund Management Guide

## Overview
Process item returns, partial or full refunds, store credits, and credit note journal postings.

## Workflows
- **Return Processing**: Scan sale receipt barcode to pull up original cart items.
- **Refund Methods**: Original payment method refund or store credit voucher issuance.
- **Inventory Restock**: Optional automated restock of returned items back into store inventory.
