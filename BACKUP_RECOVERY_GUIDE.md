# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Backup & Disaster Recovery Guide

## Overview
Automated full, incremental, and differential database & storage backups with AES-256 encryption and cryptographic integrity verification.

## Backup Schedules
- **Full System Backup**: Daily at 03:00 AM UTC.
- **Incremental Transaction Log Snapshots**: Hourly.
- **Differential Backups**: Every 6 hours.
