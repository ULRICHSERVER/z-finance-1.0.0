# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Omnichannel Marketing Automation Guide

## Overview
Drip campaign execution across Email, SMS, Push, and WhatsApp with customer segment rules and engagement analytics.

## Features
- **Campaign Channels**: Email, SMS, Push, WhatsApp.
- **Audience Segmentation**: Target by customer category, loyalty tier, or subscription plan.
- **Analytics**: Open rate, click-through rate, and lead conversion reporting.
