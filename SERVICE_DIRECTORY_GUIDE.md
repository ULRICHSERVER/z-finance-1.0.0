# Service Directory Guide - Z-FINANCE 1.0.0

## 1. Overview
The **Public Service Directory** provides a customer-facing marketplace view of all public services configured in Z-FINANCE.

---

## 2. Visibility Levels
- **Public**: Searchable in marketplace directory.
- **Private**: Accessible via direct link/contract only.
- **Hidden**: Internal organizational service catalog.

---

## 3. SEO & Open Graph Support
Each service stores custom `seo_title`, `meta_description`, `keywords`, and `og_image` for Google search index optimization.
