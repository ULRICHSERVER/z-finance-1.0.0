# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Affiliate & Partner Referral Program Guide

## Overview
Partner referral link generation, click/conversion tracking, multi-tier commission structures, and automated payout requests.

## Features
- **Affiliate Links**: Unique tracking URLs per referral partner.
- **Commission Calculations**: Fixed fee or percentage-based commission rules.
- **Payout Ledger**: Automated GL credit journal entries upon payout approval.
