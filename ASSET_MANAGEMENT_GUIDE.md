# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Enterprise Asset & Equipment Management Guide

## Overview
The Asset Management module enables enterprise tracking of physical machinery, IT hardware, corporate vehicles, furniture, and laboratory equipment.

## Key Capabilities
- **Asset Registration**: Unique asset tag IDs, serial numbers, purchase cost, and salvage value.
- **Resource Allocation**: Assign assets directly to Employees, Departments, Projects, or Locations.
- **Condition & Status Monitoring**: Track asset health (`excellent`, `good`, `fair`, `needs_repair`) and status (`in_use`, `available`, `under_maintenance`, `disposed`).
