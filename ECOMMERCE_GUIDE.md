# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - eCommerce & Web Storefront Guide

## Overview
Web store catalog management with real-time stock synchronization across physical stores and warehouse inventory.

## Features
- **Storefront Catalog**: Categories, brands, customer reviews, and wishlist.
- **Guest & Account Checkout**: Frictionless checkout experience integrated with Stripe, PayPal, and Mobile Money.
- **Auto Inventory Sync**: Online sales immediately reduce warehouse stock across the ERP system.
