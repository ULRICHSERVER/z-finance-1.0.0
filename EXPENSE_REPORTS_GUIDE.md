# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 — Expense Reports & Analytics Guide

## Overview

The **Expense Analytics Engine** transforms raw expenditure records into strategic cost-reduction insights and financial statements.

---

## Generated Reports

1. **Daily & Weekly Expense Statements**: Tactical cash disbursement tracking.
2. **Monthly & Quarterly Expense Reports**: Strategic cost trends for executive reviews.
3. **Expense by Category**: Breakdown across Rent, Office, SaaS, Telecom, Travel, and Marketing.
4. **Expense by Supplier**: Key vendor spend and negotiation insights.
5. **Expense by Project & Workspace**: Cost accounting for client projects.

---

## API Request Example

```http
GET /modules/expenses/api/reports.php?type=category&start_date=2026-01-01&end_date=2026-12-31
```
