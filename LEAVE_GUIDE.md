# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Leave Management & Workflows Guide

## Overview
Comprehensive leave request submission, approval chains, and balance tracking.

## Workflows
- **Leave Types**: Annual, Sick, Maternity, Paternity, Study, Compassionate, Unpaid.
- **Accrual Logic**: Auto-calculated leave entitlement per employment duration.
- **Approval Chains**: Multi-tier review (Line Manager -> HR Manager).
- **Calendar Integration**: Auto-populates company master calendar with employee leave dates.
