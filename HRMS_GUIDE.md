# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Enterprise HRMS & Payroll Guide

## Overview
The Z-FINANCE Human Resources Management System (HRMS) provides end-to-end employee lifecycle, recruitment applicant tracking, QR/Biometric attendance tracking, leave request management, bulk monthly payroll execution, 360° performance reviews, training matrix, and Employee Self-Service (ESS).

## System Capabilities
1. **HR Dashboard**: Key metrics on workforce headcount, daily attendance, pending leave, open job vacancies, and monthly payroll budget spend.
2. **Employee Lifecycle**: Recruitment, onboarding, probation, promotion, transfer, suspension, termination, exit interviews, and offboarding.
3. **QR & Biometric Attendance**: Scan-to-check-in, shift schedules, late arrival logging, and offline local check-in queuing.
4. **Leave Management**: Leave accrual, annual/sick/maternity leave balances, multi-tier approval workflows, and leave calendar view.
5. **Bulk Payroll & General Ledger Integration**: Automated gross-to-net salary calculation, statutory tax & pension deductions, automated General Ledger journal posting, and PDF payslips.
6. **Employee Self-Service (ESS)**: Self-service portal for employees to view profiles, download payslips, request leave, and access personal attendance QR codes.
7. **Super Admin HR Control**: Total policy override, payroll period locking/unlocking, and policy rule configuration.
