# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal Guide

## Overview
Secure employee self-service portal giving staff control over their personal records, payslips, leave requests, and attendance QR codes.

## Capabilities
- **Payslip Downloads**: Immediate access to PDF monthly payslips.
- **Leave Requests**: One-click leave application submission with instant status updates.
- **Attendance QR**: Digital barcode/QR badge for physical building entry.
- **Profile Updates**: Contact information and banking detail submission.
