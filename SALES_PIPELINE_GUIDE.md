# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Sales Pipeline & Deal Management Guide

## Overview
Visual pipeline tracking from deal lead qualification to win/loss closing, quote generation, and automated invoice conversion.

## Features
- **Stages**: Lead -> Qualified -> Proposal -> Negotiation -> Won -> Lost / Cancelled.
- **Probability Scoring**: Win probability percentage mapped to weighted forecast revenue.
- **Quotation & Proposal Sync**: One-click proposal generation and conversion to Billing invoices.
