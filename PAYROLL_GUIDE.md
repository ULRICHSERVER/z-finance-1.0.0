# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Payroll & General Ledger Integration Guide

## Overview
The Payroll Engine automates gross-to-net calculations, tax & pension statutory deductions, loan repayments, and GL journal posting.

## Key Features
- **Pay Grades & Basic Salary**: Configurable per department and job level.
- **Statutory Deductions**: 10% income tax and 8% pension auto-deductions.
- **Bulk Processing**: One-click bulk payroll execution for all active workforce.
- **General Ledger Sync**: Automatically creates GL debit entries for salary expense and credit entries for cash/bank liabilities.
- **Payslips**: Digitally generated PDF payslips delivered directly to ESS.
