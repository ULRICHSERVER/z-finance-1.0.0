# Z-FINANCE 1.0.0 - Membership & VIP Portal Guide

## Overview
Enterprise membership administration for individual, corporate, association, and VIP tier members.

## Features
- **Membership Types**: Individual, Corporate, Association, Student, VIP.
- **Digital Cards**: Automated digital membership badge issuance.
- **Expiration Alerts**: Automated email/SMS notifications 30/15/7 days prior to expiry.
